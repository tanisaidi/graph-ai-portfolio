About Me

My Journey

Hello! I am a GIS Specialist and Full-Stack Software Engineer based in Harare, Zimbabwe. With an Honors Degree in Surveying and Geomatics from Midlands State University, my career has evolved from intricate land surveying to architecting complex spatial data models and digital solutions.

Currently, I serve as a GIS Specialist at the World Health Organization (WHO), leveraging geospatial intelligence to support critical global initiatives. Prior to this, I spent years at ZETDC leading system upgrades, integrating smart metering networks, and developing enterprise web/mobile applications. I am deeply passionate about utilizing Generative AI to optimize development workflows and enhance analytical capabilities.
